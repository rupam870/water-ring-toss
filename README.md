# Water Ring Toss — under the stars

A browser remake of the childhood handheld water ring toss toy. You're lying out under a starry sky with trees on both sides, and two cousins, Mira and little Kabir, lean over the toy, cheering every ring that lands, giggling at every one that bounces off, and asking for a turn. Their pictures were made with Atlabs.

**Play:** https://rupam.work/water-ring-toss/

## How to play

- **Pump:** tap a yellow button for a gentle puff, or hold it for a big push. Letting go refills the bellows and pulls water back towards the nozzle.
- **Tilt:** on a phone, tilt the phone itself (on iPhone, tap "Tap to tilt with your phone" once to allow motion access). On a computer, drag the toy to rotate it, or use the ← → keys.
- **Keys:** A / L to pump, R to reset.

Pick a mode: **Easy** (6 big rings, about a minute), **Medium** (10 rings, about 5 minutes) or **Hard** (12 rings, the real toy). Land every ring for a star rating, then share your card. Hard presses leave the water swirling for a few seconds and can knock rings back off.

It's a single `index.html` with no build step. The water and sounds are all drawn and synthesised in the browser.
