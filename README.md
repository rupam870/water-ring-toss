# Water Ring Toss — under the stars

A browser remake of the childhood handheld water ring toss toy. You're lying out under a starry sky with trees on both sides, and Pip, a little astronaut who fell from the stars, leans over the toy, cheering every ring that lands, groaning at every one that falls off, and asking for a turn.

**Play:** https://rupam870.github.io/water-ring-toss/

## How to play

- **Pump:** tap a yellow button for a gentle puff, or hold it for a big push. Letting go refills the bellows and pulls water back towards the nozzle.
- **Tilt:** on a phone, tilt the phone itself (on iPhone, tap "Tap to tilt with your phone" once to allow motion access). On a computer, drag the toy to rotate it, or use the ← → keys.
- **Keys:** A / L to pump, R to reset.

Pick a mode: **Easy** (6 rings, the pegs help you, about a minute), **Medium** (10 rings, a little help, about 5 minutes) or **Hard** (12 rings, the real toy). Land every ring for a star rating, then share your card. Hard presses leave the water swirling for a few seconds and can knock rings back off.

It's a single `index.html` with no build step. The water, sounds and Pip are all drawn and synthesised in the browser.
