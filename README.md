# Water Ring Toss — under the stars

A browser remake of the childhood handheld water ring toss toy. You're lying out under a starry sky with trees on both sides, and your little brother is leaning over the toy, cheering every ring that lands, groaning at every one that falls off, and asking when it's his turn.

**Play:** https://rupam870.github.io/water-ring-toss/

## How to play

- **Pump:** tap a yellow button for a gentle puff, or hold it for a big push. Letting go refills the bellows and pulls water back towards the nozzle.
- **Tilt:** on a phone, tilt the phone itself (on iPhone, tap "Tap to tilt with your phone" once to allow motion access). On a computer, drag the toy to rotate it, or use the ← → keys.
- **Keys:** A / L to pump, R to reset.

Get all 12 rings onto the two pegs. Hard presses leave the water swirling for a few seconds and can knock rings back off.

It's a single `index.html` with no build step. The water, sounds and the brother are all drawn and synthesised in the browser.
